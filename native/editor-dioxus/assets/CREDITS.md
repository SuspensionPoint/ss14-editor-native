# Credits for the art in this folder

## The clown

`clown.png` is the clown that hops across the loading screen. The app icon (`icon.png` and
`icon.ico`) is made from it: cropped, centred on a square and scaled up.

We didn't record exactly where the clown came from. It is credited as art from Space Station
14 (https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14) and its contributors, licensed under
CC-BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The icon files are shared under
the same licence. If you know who drew it, please open an issue so the credit can name them.
